Call Sheet Builder

Ohio State Playbook · College Football 27

Tap + to add a play. Tap a type label or a badge to change it.

Plays marked with › need a shift or motion before the snap. Type how you do it for each one.

Plays come from the Ohio State playbook only. Run, pass, PA and RPO labels, plus the F/B, C, BB, T and › badges, are first guesses worked out from the play names. Fix any of them in the Playbook tab. Tap ★ in the Edit tab to mark your own off-script calls.

Sheets save in this browser on this device. Print / PDF prints the front and back as two landscape pages, where you can choose Save as PDF. Save image makes a picture of the side you are viewing, and Export CSV downloads the calls. Fan-made tool, not affiliated with EA Sports, Ohio State or College Football 27.
