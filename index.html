Call Sheet Builder

Ohio State Playbook · College Football 27

Tap + to add a play. Tap a type label to change it.

Plays come from the Ohio State playbook only, not every play in the game. Run, pass, PA and RPO labels are inferred from play names, so some may be off.

Sheets save in this browser on this device. Print / PDF opens your print screen, where you can choose Save as PDF. Save image makes a picture of the sheet, and Export CSV downloads the calls. Fan-made tool, not affiliated with EA Sports, Ohio State or College Football 27.
